Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: seamless 360 degree equirectangular environment panorama for a fixed-position first-person WebGL surface observatory. The image will wrap around the viewer; it is a texture asset, not a poster.
Primary request: a scientifically informed artistic reconstruction of standing 1.65 meters above Jupiter's moon Europa. Desolate frozen pale ivory and grey water-ice terrain with subdued rusty brown mineral seams, low irregular pressure ridges, fractured slabs, granular frost, and a sinuous shallow fissure leading from the foreground into the distance. Photorealistic spacecraft expedition photography, sober natural materials, detailed but believable scale. No towering fantasy mountains, no enormous chasms, no blue glowing ice.
Projection and framing: full spherical equirectangular 360 degrees longitude by 180 degrees latitude, 2:1 image. The horizon is exactly halfway down the image, with low ice ridges only a little above it. Entire top half is perfectly uniform pure black empty sky; lower half contains terrain all the way to the bottom nadir. Horizontally seamless left and right edges, consistent perspective around the full sphere, no panels or borders. Center of image looks at a wide open ice plain with a fine fissure and textured ice plates; left and right have modest low pressure ridges. Bottom edge converges naturally at the nadir.
Lighting: a single low distant sun at about 12 degrees elevation, azimuth 70 degrees clockwise from the center viewing direction; sun itself must not appear. Hard low-angle sunlight from the right in the central view, long natural shadows toward the left, gentle dark reflected fill. Neutral ivory sunlight; physically plausible cold vacuum landscape. No atmosphere or fog.
Text: none.
Constraints: render terrain only and uniform pure black sky. NO Jupiter, NO planets, NO moons, NO sun disc, NO stars, NO galaxy, NO spacecraft, NO people, NO helmet, NO buildings, NO plants, NO water, NO snowflakes, NO text, NO UI, NO watermark.
